Make the site counters real and let the operator pick a giveaway prize

The footer read 3 869 online / 412 589 players / 1 542 380 cases / 894 120
upgrades. Those were literals behind a sine wave, so the online badge showed
thousands of visitors to a site nobody had signed up for. They are now summed
from the data the server holds, with online counting open sockets. Cached for
four seconds because every websocket tick asked for them.

The top drop and the winner list were full of people who never played: every
request carrying a bare caser_user_id cookie provisions a player, and
seed-demo.js invents a dozen pro nicknames on a cold start. Three changes:

* The demo roster is no longer seeded on a deployed host (SEED_DEMO=1 puts it
  back), so it stops coming back on every restart.
* purgeTestUsers removes those accounts together with their drops, entries and
  stats, exposed in the panel behind a dry-run. It refuses to touch an account
  that came from a legacy slug - that is a browser with a real session, the
  operator's among them - or one with an admin role.
* The giveaway banner re-checks itself every 30s. A round settled early by the
  operator never reached zero on its countdown, so the banner kept advertising
  a prize that was already gone.

When the last round finishes the bundle renders no giveaway section at all and
the column was blank for three cards' height; it now says so instead.

The prize picker searches the catalogue and fills in the image, the price and
the rarity tint, instead of asking the operator to type a name exactly and
trusting the banner to render something. A name that matches nothing is still
offered as-is, since cases are not in the skin catalogue. One winner by
default; a round can still be given more slots.

"Продать все" now sits directly left of the sort select using `order` rather
than a wrapper node - React kept re-rendering the toolbar and putting the
button back in the middle of the row.

Also: id_aliases has no `id` column (it is keyed by old_id, and new_id is what
has to go), a purge is chunked because SQLite caps bound variables, and
requireAdmin no longer depends on the alias row existing to recognise the
operator's own account.
